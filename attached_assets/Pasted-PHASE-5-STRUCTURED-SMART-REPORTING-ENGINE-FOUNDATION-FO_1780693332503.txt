PHASE 5 – STRUCTURED SMART REPORTING ENGINE (FOUNDATION FOR FUTURE AI)

Objective:
Build a deterministic, rules-based reporting engine that generates radiology findings and impressions from structured selections. This is NOT AI. It must be auditable, editable, explainable, and production-safe. Future AI modules will use this structured layer as their grounding source.

====================================================
1. NEW MODULE: SMART FINDINGS
====================================================

Add a new toolbar button in Radiology Report page:

Smart Findings

When clicked, open a side panel/drawer.

The panel should generate findings text dynamically from structured selections.

All generated text remains fully editable.

6. RULE-BASED IMPRESSION GENERATOR
====================================================

Button:

Generate Impression

Rules Engine Examples:

IF:
Disc Bulge + Nerve Root Compression

THEN:
"Diffuse disc bulge causing compression upon bilateral exiting nerve roots."

----------------------------------------------------

IF:
Acute Infarct + MCA Territory

THEN:
"Acute infarct involving the MCA territory."

----------------------------------------------------

IF:
EDH

THEN:
"Extradural hematoma with associated mass effect."

----------------------------------------------------

IF:
Grade 3 Fatty Liver

THEN:
"Severe fatty infiltration of liver."

Rules must be editable via admin panel.
