Build an advanced Echocardiography and Fetal Echocardiography reporting automation module inside my existing ERP/RIS/PACS system.

Environment:

- Existing ERP
- Conquest PACS
- Weasis Viewer
- Existing radiology worklist
- Existing patient registration, billing, reporting system

Goal:
Create a cardiology reporting workflow where Echo and Fetal Echo studies are automatically received, measurements extracted, structured reports generated, AI-assisted impressions created, reviewed by doctor, finalized, digitally signed, archived, and delivered to patient.

Important:
AI must NEVER independently diagnose.
AI assists only.
Final report requires doctor review and sign-off.
